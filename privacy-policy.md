# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Islam Habits is an Android app for prayer times, prayer focus tracking, duas,
tasbih, dhikr, Qibla direction, the Qur'an, the 99 Names of Allah, and embedded
educational videos. It is published by Coded Dreams.

## Contact

For privacy questions, write to **codeddreams.app@gmail.com**.

## The Short Version

- There is no account to create, and no sign-in.
- The developer runs no server that receives your data, and the app has no
  analytics or crash reporting of its own.
- Your prayer records, counts, progress, search history and saved location stay
  on your device.
- Some features contact other companies directly, and those companies see your
  IP address and device details: Google (ads, purchases, YouTube videos and
  place-name lookup), the Islamic Network (Qur'an recitation audio) and GitHub
  (the list of video links). Details are below.
- Islam Habits does not sell personal data.

## What Stays On Your Device

Islam Habits stores the following on your device only:

- your prayer and Tahajjud focus records, dua and tasbih counts, Qur'an and 99
  Names progress and daily dhikr;
- your Qur'an search history (the words you searched for);
- your saved prayer location, and the prayer times calculated from it;
- your theme, alert settings, dua reminders and tutorial progress;
- a record of the ad-free purchase, if you have made one (see Purchases).

**Location.** Islam Habits asks for your location when you choose to set or
update your prayer location, and takes a single position fix, precise or
approximate depending on the permission you grant. It does not track your
location in the background. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated
on the device from the saved position and are never requested from a server.
The coordinates are sent once to Android's geocoding service, which on most
devices is provided by Google, to look up a place name to display. If that lookup
fails, the saved location is shown as coordinates instead.

## What Leaves Your Device, And Where It Goes

Whenever the app talks to another company's servers, that company receives your
IP address and basic device and app details, as with any internet connection.

| Feature | Who receives data | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| Ads | Google (AdMob) | Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics |
| Ad-free purchase | Google Play | Purchase and account details handled by Google Play; a random install ID |
| YouTube videos | YouTube / Google | IP address, device details, video and playback information, cookies or similar data in the app's web view |
| Qur'an recitation | Islamic Network CDN | The ayah, reciter and audio quality you play, plus IP address and device type |
| Video links | GitHub Pages (GitHub) | IP address and device and app details, like any web request; nothing else is sent |
| Place-name lookup | Android geocoding service (usually Google) | Your coordinates, once, when you set your location |

## Ads And Consent

Islam Habits shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising
ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads to show
and measure ads, including personalised ads where you allow it, and may receive
crash and performance diagnostics from the ads software, under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app declares the
`AD_ID` permission so that the advertising ID can be used.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for
your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested.
On any device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation
in the device's Google settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).

## Purchases

The optional ad-free purchase (a subscription or a one-time purchase) is handled
entirely by Google Play. Islam Habits never sees your payment details. So that the
purchase can be restored and checked, the app keeps the Google Play purchase
details (including the purchase token, product and plan) and a random install ID
on your device, signed with a key held in the Android Keystore, and verifies the
purchase with Google Play. The developer runs no server for this. This record is
excluded from backup and from device-to-device transfer.

## YouTube Videos

Islam Habits embeds videos using YouTube's embedded player and API services. The
player and its script are loaded from YouTube (youtube.com and
youtube-nocookie.com). When you view or interact with a video, YouTube and Google
may collect and process data, and may store cookies or similar data in the app's
built-in web view, according to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google
Privacy Policy.

Official links:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- YouTube API Services Terms: https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/api-services-terms-of-service

Islam Habits does not remove, block, or obscure YouTube player controls,
branding, ads, or links supplied by the embedded player.

## Video Links

The list of YouTube links the app shows is a small public file hosted on GitHub
Pages (coded-dreams.github.io). So that a broken video can be replaced without an
app update, the app downloads that file when it starts and, if it has been open for
a while, again at most once an hour, and keeps the last copy on your device. The
request carries no account, identifier or personal information. GitHub, which hosts
the file, sees your IP address and device and app details like any web server, under
its own privacy statement; the developer receives nothing from these requests.

## Qur'an Recitation Audio

The Qur'an text is bundled with the app and is read offline. Recitation audio is
streamed on demand from the Islamic Network CDN (https://cdn.islamic.network)
when you play an ayah. The request names the ayah, reciter and audio quality,
and the CDN also sees your IP address and device type. The audio is never stored
on your device.

## Alerts And Reminders

Prayer alerts and dua reminders require permission to post notifications, and
use exact alarms so that they arrive at the correct time. They may vibrate, and
they are rescheduled after the device restarts, is updated, or has its clock or
time zone changed. Every alert is scheduled on the device from prayer times
calculated on the device.

## Backup

If Android backup is enabled for your device, your records, settings, saved
location and search history are copied to your own Google account so that a new
device can restore them. That copy is held under your Google account, not by the
developer, and can be removed in your Google Account backup settings. The ad-free
purchase record is excluded from backup and from device-to-device transfer.

## Data Retention And Deleting Your Data

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. The reset buttons on
the calendar, Dhikr and Qur'an screens clear the records they show. To remove
everything, including your saved location and search history, clear the app's
storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not remove a
cloud backup copy; delete that in your Google Account backup settings. Because the
developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from the
developer; data held by Google, YouTube, GitHub or the Islamic Network is governed by their
own policies.

## Children

Islam Habits is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
their personal information.

## Security

Network communication with Google, YouTube, GitHub and the Islamic Network CDN
uses HTTPS. Local app data is stored in Android app storage controlled by the
operating system. The purchase record is signed with a key held in the Android
Keystore.

## Data Safety

The Google Play Data safety section for Islam Habits reflects the use of
location, ads and advertising identifiers, purchases, embedded YouTube video
playback and streamed recitation audio described above, together with the
records stored only on your device.
